Vollständige Beschreibung

As a Senior Channel Partner Manager, you will proactively contribute to and lead work packages that build, drive, and grow an effective network of agents, resellers and lead finders as well as a global channel partner program. By doing so, you will help increase market reach, accelerate market penetration, generate additional revenue and profit for the Group, and ultimately enhance customer satisfaction.

Aufgaben

  • You define, together with the team, the missing elements of a next-level Channel Partner program framework
  • You devise, deploy, and maintain a structured, coherent, Group-wide end-to-end process for Channel Partner Management, outlining the methodologies, detailed steps, procedures, and measurements needed to recruit, onboard, develop, review, measure, manage, and offboard channel partners at Bühler
  • You engage and interact regularly with cross-regional, cross-Business Area, and cross-departmental stakeholders
  • You act as Deputy Head of Group Channel Partner Management

Anforderungen

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree, or equivalent qualifications, in business administration, management, or a similar field
  • 5-10 years of experience in managing channel partners such as agents and resellers in a B2B environment, preferably in mechanical engineering or the plant-building industry
  • Experience in leading a team or department, preferably in an international work environment
  • Knowledge of devising go-to-market and route-to-market strategies, with a particular focus on indirect sales channels
  • Entrepreneurial spirit, strong networking ability, and very good communication skills in English; other languages are a plus
