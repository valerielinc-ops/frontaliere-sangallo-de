Vollständige Beschreibung

As an Intern CRM Transformation / Program Facilitator, you will work at the intersection of business, technology, data and change management. You will gain insight into an international, company-wide transformation and support the program with both conceptual and analytical tasks as well as operational and administrative project work.

Aufgaben

  • You support the planning and implementation of change management, communication and user enablement activities
  • You organize workshops, project meetings and other program activities and help with their content preparation and follow-up
  • You take part in testing the new CRM solution and help document results and identified improvement opportunities in a structured way
  • You contribute to general project tasks and gradually take on responsibility for your own work packages
  • Depending on your interests and the current program status, you can dive deeper into topics such as sales and service processes, analytics, change management, rollout or project steering

Anforderungen

  • Ongoing or recently completed studies in Business Administration, Business Informatics, General Management, Data Science, Industrial Engineering or a comparable field
  • Fluent English, both spoken and written, as English is the main project language
  • Conceptual and analytical thinking, a proactive, pragmatic and self-driven working style, and the ability to present complex content in a clear and audience-appropriate way
  • Willingness to reliably handle both challenging content-related tasks and necessary operational and administrative project work
  • Interest in digital transformation, CRM, data, sales and service processes, and change management
